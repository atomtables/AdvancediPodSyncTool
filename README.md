# Advanced iPod Sync Tool
sync stuff that isn't music, video, or photo to and from your 2012 macbook air compatibles with Es.

iTunes doesn't seem to let you sync extras really easily. You have to manually copy over Calendars, Contacts, and Notes (even splitting them up) if you want them to sync. Now let's say I really like reading through articles on a 2" screen. What do I do, aside from spending an hour of my day splitting a text into 4000-character BS?

spend 40x the hours on making this app!!!

Plug in an iPod and it'll appear in the side panel. When you click it, the calendars will populate, and you can select which ones. 

Planned features:
- You can include existing text files for some articles, or just directly download from existing RSS feeds
- Your contacts will automatically update onto your iPod
